Ontology Reference

Jobs-to-be-Done v3 — Node & Edge Types

The 5-level causal chain ontology used by the interview system.

Node Types by Level
Level 0
job_context
Situation or circumstances when the job arises
job_trigger
Event or stimulus that initiates the job
Level 1
pain_point
Obstacle or frustration in getting the job done
gain_point
Desired outcome or benefit that makes the job worthwhile
Level 2
job_statement
Core functional job a customer is trying to accomplish
Level 3
emotional_job
Emotional state or identity the customer seeks to achieve
social_job
Social impression or relationship the customer wants to influence
Level 4
solution_approach
What the customer currently hires to make progress — current or potential solution
Edge Types
Chain drivers
triggers
Causal: source initiates or causes target (situation gives rise to need)
implies
Need reveals or defines the job (pain/gain implies what the functional job is)
supports
Job co-occurs with or reinforces emotional/social motivation (non-causal)
drives
Motivation or job shapes solution selection (biases tradeoffs, motivates hiring)
Supporting
occurs_in
Trigger occurs within a specific context (situational containment)
addresses
Solution resolves a pain point (downward edge, not chain progression)
achieves
Solution delivers a gain point (downward edge, not chain progression)
conflicts_with
Something that prevents or undermines something else
revises
Contradiction — newer belief or approach supersedes older one
