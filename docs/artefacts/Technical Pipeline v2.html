Interview engine · turn pipeline

Inside one turn — listen & gather, then understand & act

A respondent answer enters; a grounded next question leaves. Two phases: during phase 1 the engine listens to the answer and grows the conversation map; in phase 2 the engine reads the map, picks a move, writes the question, persists state.
