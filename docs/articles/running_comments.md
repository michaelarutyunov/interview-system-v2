**Best: The social normalisation chain (Full chains 1–2)**

This is the strongest illustration in any session so far. A standard beverage research guide would ask about taste, caffeine, sugar content, convenience. It demonstrates the article's core claim about late-emerging motivational structure that no script would reach, and it's a clean, well-evidenced chain with no weak cross-turn edges.

**Good: The competence/preparation chain (Full chain 9)**

`missing meeting decisions and needing to catch up secondhand → staying informed and in the loop → feel competent and on top of things at work → grabbing a drink before meetings start`

This one illustrates the structural connection the graph makes explicit — that a drink-grabbing behaviour is structurally connected to a professional identity concern. The connection spans turns 5 and 8 and wouldn't be visible in the transcript without the graph. **Also usable: The physical dehydration trigger (Advanced chain 20)**

Shorter chain, easier to show in prose.
